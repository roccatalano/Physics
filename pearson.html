What Pearson adds

Pracs (SAB SPARKlabs)

23 practical activities, PA01 to PA23, with teacher notes, solutions and risk assessments per AoS. They overlap Cambridge's pracs in places (e.g. series and parallel circuits, specific heat) and add new ones: speed of light, G-M tube, OPAL reactor, Van de Graaff, reaction timer, g at Earth's surface, momentum and energy conservation.

Online experiments

22 online experiments, many with teacher notes and risk assessments: data analysis tasks, research topics and simple at-home or in-class experiments. Useful as prac alternatives or homework.

Videos

14 short videos already on the drive, each mapped to a Cambridge section.

Fully worked solutions

Per chapter, per AoS review and per unit. Teacher only.

SAB answers

Worksheet and exam-question solutions for each AoS. Students do not have the SAB, so these answer questions students cannot see.

Unit 2 options

Pearson adds three options Cambridge does not cover: 2.2 fusion vs fission, 2.5 forces on the human body, 2.12 AC to DC. It also has its own versions of 2.10, 2.11 and 2.15.

Unit 2 investigation

Logbook template, poster template, sample logbook and poster, student checklist, assessment rubric and teacher notes. Cambridge has nothing here, so this fills a real gap.

Course-wide

Formula and data sheet, a Units 1 and 2 practice exam with a mapping document and answers, sample teaching plans for both units, risk assessment templates, and lists of all pracs and videos.
