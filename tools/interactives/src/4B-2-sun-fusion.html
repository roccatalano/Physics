The Sun's core fuses hydrogen into helium in three steps. Four protons go in, one helium-4 nucleus comes out, and it is a little lighter than the four protons were.

Proton–proton chain

1 ${}^{1}_{1}\text{H} + {}^{1}_{1}\text{H} \rightarrow {}^{2}_{1}\text{H} + {}^{0}_{1}\text{e} + \nu$
2 ${}^{2}_{1}\text{H} + {}^{1}_{1}\text{H} \rightarrow {}^{3}_{2}\text{He} + \gamma$
3 ${}^{3}_{2}\text{He} + {}^{3}_{2}\text{He} \rightarrow {}^{4}_{2}\text{He} + {}^{1}_{1}\text{H} + {}^{1}_{1}\text{H}$
