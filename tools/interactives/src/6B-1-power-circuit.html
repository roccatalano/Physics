Kitchen power points are wired in parallel; the circuit breaker is in series in the active. What happens as you switch more on?

Kitchen power circuit (230 V)

Each appliance gets the full 230 V and draws $I = \dfrac{P}{V}$. The branch currents add up in the active wire, so the breaker trips if the total passes its rating.
