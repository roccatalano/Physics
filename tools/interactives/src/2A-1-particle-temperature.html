A sealed box of gas sits on a heater. Turn the heater on or off and watch the particles. What does a thermometer really measure?

Inside the box

Hotter means the particles move faster on average: more random kinetic energy. Temperature tracks this. $T(\text{K}) = T(^\circ\text{C}) + 273.15$, and at 0 K the motion is at its minimum.
