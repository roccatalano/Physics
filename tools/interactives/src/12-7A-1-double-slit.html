Light from one laser passes through two narrow slits. The two waves overlap on a screen. Bright bands are where they arrive in phase, dark bands where they arrive out of phase.

Top view (not to scale) and the pattern on the screen

Bright band: path difference $= n\lambda$. Dark band: path difference $= \left(n + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$.
