Straight wavefronts in a ripple tank reach a gap in a barrier. How much do they spread out after the gap? Compare the wavelength $\lambda$ with the gap width $w$.

Ripple tank, top view

The bigger the ratio $\lambda / w$, the more the waves diffract: clear diffraction when $\lambda / w$ is close to 1, very little when $\lambda / w \ll 1$. Dashed lines: edge of the central band.
