A hot block is dropped into a cup of cool water. What final temperature do they both reach?

Temperature against time

Heat flows from hot to cold until the temperatures are equal (thermal equilibrium). With no losses, energy lost by the hot object = energy gained by the water: $m_1c_1\Delta T_1 = m_2c_2\Delta T_2$.
