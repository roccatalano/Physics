Temperature against time

$Q$ energy (J), $m$ mass (kg), $c$ specific heat capacity (J/(kg K)), $\Delta T$ temperature change (°C or K, the same size), $P$ power (W), $t$ time (s).
$Q = mc\Delta T$ and $Q = Pt$, so $Pt = mc\Delta T$, so $\frac{\Delta T}{t} = \frac{P}{mc}$, so $c = \frac{P}{m \times \text{gradient}}$
