Density

Density is mass per unit volume:
$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$
where $\rho$ is the density in kg/m³, $m$ is the mass in kg and $V$ is the volume in m³.

A less dense fluid is pushed up through a denser one. A denser fluid sinks. Gravity drives both.
