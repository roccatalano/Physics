Name the three ways thermal energy moves.
Which one can cross empty space?
Conduction, convection, radiation.
Only radiation can cross space.
A black car and a white car sit in the sun.
Which one gets hotter? Why?
The black car.
Black absorbs more radiation. White reflects more.

Blackbodies
All objects above 0 K radiate.
Blackbody: absorbs all radiation
and radiates as well as possible.
Same $T$: same curve, any material.
Wien's law
$\lambda_{max}$ = peak wavelength (m)
$T$ = temperature in kelvin (K)
$b$ = Wien's constant
$b = 2.898 \times 10^{-3}$ m K
$\lambda_{max}T = b$$\lambda_{max} = \dfrac{b}{T}$
Hotter: shorter peak wavelength.
Comparing curves
Total energy = area under curve.
Hotter: much more area.
Sun 5778 K: peak 500 nm, visible.
Earth 288 K: peak 10 µm, infrared.

F$\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\lambda_{max}\)} = \dfrac{b}{\colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(T\)}}$

S$\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\lambda_{max}\)} = \dfrac{2.898 \times 10^{-3}}{\colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(523\)}}$
S$\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\lambda_{max}\)} = 5.54 \times 10^{-6}$ m
Peak at $5.54 \times 10^{-6}$ m: infrared.

In = out: temperature steady.

Surface: about 400 W/m² of infrared.
About 40 W/m² escapes through
the atmospheric window.
Greenhouse gases: H₂O, CO₂, CH₄.
They absorb and re-emit infrared.
Some comes back down: back radiation.
More gas: more back radiation: warmer.
