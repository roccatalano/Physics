Light in air hits the surface of water at 55° to the normal. Light travels at $2.25 \times 10^8$ m/s in water. Find the angle of refraction.
what we wantfind this first
VValues
$\theta_1 = 55°$   $n_1 = 1.00$   $v_2 = 2.25 \times 10^8\ \text{m/s}$   $\theta_2$ $= \ ?$
FFormula
$n_1 \sin\theta_1 =$ $n_2$ $\sin$ $\theta_2$
First find $n_2$
$n_2$ $= \dfrac{c}{v_2} = \dfrac{3.00 \times 10^8}{2.25 \times 10^8} =$ $1.33$
SSubstitute
$1.00 \times \sin 55° =$ $1.33$ $\times \sin$ $\theta_2$
SSolve
$\sin$ $\theta_2$ $= \dfrac{0.819}{1.33} = 0.616$   $\theta_2$ $= 38°$
The ray bends towards the normal, to $38°$.
Light in air hits a glass block ($n = 1.50$) at 30° to the normal. Find the angle of refraction.
V$n_1 = 1.00$   $\theta_1 = 30°$   $n_2 = 1.50$
F$n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$
S$1.00 \times \sin 30° = 1.50 \times \sin\theta_2$
S$\sin\theta_2 = 0.333$   $\theta_2 = 19.5°$
Light travels at $2.00 \times 10^8$ m/s in a glass block. A ray inside the block meets the surface at 25° to the normal. Find its angle in air.
V$\theta_1 = 25°$   $v_1 = 2.00 \times 10^8$ m/s   $n_2 = 1.00$
F$n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$   first: $n_1 = \dfrac{3.00}{2.00} = 1.50$
S$1.50 \times \sin 25° = 1.00 \times \sin\theta_2$

The line of best fit evens out the errors. Its gradient is $n$.

what we wantfind this first

FFormula

Refraction
Refraction: light changes direction
because it changes speed.
Into slower: bends towards the normal.
Into faster: bends away from the normal.
Refractive index
$n$ = refractive index (no unit)
$v$ = speed of light in the material (m/s)
$c$ = $3.00 \times 10^8$ m/s, light in a vacuum
$n = \dfrac{c}{v}$$n_1v_1 = n_2v_2$
Bigger $n$ = slower light.
e.g. air 1.00, water 1.33, glass 1.50
$f$ stays the same. $\lambda$ gets shorter.
Snell's law
$\theta$ = angle between ray and normal
1 = first material, 2 = second material
$n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$
Worked example 1
Air into water at 55° to the normal.
Light in water: $2.25 \times 10^8$ m/s.
Find the angle of refraction.
V$\theta_1 = 55°$, $n_1 = 1.00$,
$v_2 = 2.25 \times 10^8$ m/s
F$n_1\sin\theta_1 = \colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(n_2\)}\sin\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\theta_2\)}$
$\colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(n_2\)} = \dfrac{3.00 \times 10^8}{2.25 \times 10^8} = \colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(1.33\)}$
S$1.00 \times \sin 55° = \colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(1.33\)} \times \sin\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\theta_2\)}$
S$\sin\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\theta_2\)} = \dfrac{0.819}{\colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(1.33\)}} = 0.616$
$\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\theta_2\)} = 38°$
The ray bends to 38° from the normal.

Find the angle of refraction.
V$\theta_1 = 40°$, $n_1 = 1.00$,

F$n_1\sin\theta_1 = \colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(n_2\)}\sin\colorbox{#ffee58}{\(\theta_2\)}$
$\colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(n_2\)} = \dfrac{3.00 \times 10^8}{2.40 \times 10^8} = \colorbox{#b9f5b0}{\(1.25\)}$

Images
The eye traces rays back in straight lines.
The image is higher than the real object.
Looking straight down:
apparent depth $\approx \dfrac{\text{real depth}}{n}$
Partial internal reflection
Partial internal reflection: at a boundary,
some light reflects and the rest refracts.
Total internal reflection
Critical angle $\theta_c$: the $\theta_1$ that
gives $\theta_2 = 90°$: the ray skims along.
Total internal reflection:
all the light reflects back.
Needs high $n$ to low $n$, and $\theta_1 > \theta_c$.
$n_1\sin\theta_c = n_2$
Optical fibres
Light stays in the core by total internal
reflection. The cladding has a lower $n$.
A ray that is too steep meets the wall
at less than $\theta_c$ and leaks out.

Dispersion
Dispersion: white light splits into colours.
In glass, $n$ depends on the colour.
Violet: biggest $n$, bends most. Red: least.
Chromatic distortion: a lens focuses
each colour at a different point.
Rainbows and mirages
Rainbow: in each raindrop, light refracts
in, reflects off the back and refracts out.
See it with the Sun behind you.
Mirage: hot air near a road has a
lower $n$. Light from the sky bends
back up into your eye.
