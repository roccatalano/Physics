# Chapter resources (Year 11 Physics)

Built 19 Nov 2026 for Chapters 1 and 2 (Area of Study 1). Nothing here has been tried with a class yet.

- `ch01/`, `ch02/`: the built chapter deck (open the .html file in Chrome), the reference pages as PDFs, the "I can do this" checklist with its teacher answers, the one-page chapter summary, the corrected test solutions and teacher notes, and the practice booklet page map and teacher notes.
- `tools/`: the scripts that build the booklet, tests, checklist and summary, so later chapters can reuse them.
- The deck source is in `decks/`.

Not in this repo: the practice booklets and the student copies of the chapter tests. They are whole Cambridge documents, and this repo is public. They are on the shared drive only.
